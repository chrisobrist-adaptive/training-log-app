Intro to equipped bench press

An 8-week program for your first run at equipped benching. Enter your email and you'll get the full program straight away. It's free.

What's inside

  • 8 weeks, 4 sessions a weekTwo training blocks with a deload between them, every session laid out for you
  • Loads worked out for youYour secondary and tertiary bench days are set from your raw bench max. Your equipped day loads are generated after week 1 is logged in both blocks. Every exercise has RPE and RIR targets
  • Built for learning the equipmentEquipped bench and board presses alongside the back, triceps and accessory work that support them
  • Track your progressLog your actual loads and RPE, and the sheet tracks your estimated max and training load with graphs

Who it's for

Anyone trying equipped bench for the first time, whether you're using a slingshot for your equipped day or you've got your own bench shirt.
